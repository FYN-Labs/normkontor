# Privacy and regulated controls

Normkontor is designed to support a controlled GDPR-, DORA-, and EU-AI-Act-aware
deployment process. It is not legal advice, a certification, or a blanket
compliance declaration.

## Control set

1. **Purpose and allowlist:** named teams, repositories, paths, data classes,
   purposes, models, regions, tools, and actions.
2. **Minimization:** diff or symbol package before repository-wide context.
3. **Detection:** secrets, personal data, health, claims, employment, payment,
   and other regulated categories according to client policy.
4. **Block or redact:** block when redaction would destroy semantics or hide a
   security-relevant fact.
5. **Route proof:** record provider, resolved model, region/profile, retention,
   and fallback behavior rather than trusting a marketing label.
6. **Evidence minimization:** store policy, hashes, status, and findings by
   default; retain source or prompts only under an explicit justified policy.
7. **Human control:** no individual productivity scoring or high-stakes
   insurance decision automation without the required human review and legal
   basis.

## Organizational work that remains required

Depending on the deployment, the client may need a processing inventory,
legal-basis analysis, data-processing agreements, transfer assessment, DPIA,
works-council consultation, information-security approval, provider-risk and
subprocessor review, AI-system inventory, literacy/training evidence, incident
handling, and retention/deletion procedures.

Technical scrubbing can reduce exposure. It does not replace these decisions.

# Normkontor Academy and rollout

Status: bounded pilot formats on request; no claim of customer deployment or
measured productivity improvement.

## Six-week rollout

1. **Frame:** one team, one workflow, data classes, success question, stop
   criteria, decision owners.
2. **Configure:** agent setup, repository rules, permissions, native tools, and
   evidence path.
3. **Enable:** role-based practical training on the approved workflow.
4. **Operate:** accompanied pilot with exception, incident, and support log.
5. **Evaluate:** quality, work quality, friction, risk, cost, and open evidence.
6. **Decide:** scale, revise, or stop plus a prioritized 90-day plan.

Outputs can include a pilot charter, operating model, repository rules, risk
register, training records, eval catalog, evidence templates, and transition
plan. Exact deliverables belong in the client statement of work.

## Academy tracks

- **Executive & Governance — one day:** operating model, risk classes, human
  gates, claims, procurement, and decision authority.
- **Practitioner — two days:** Codex/agent setup, context, native tools, 3A
  ladder, tests, receipts, review practice, and safe escalation.
- **Trainer & Assurance Lead — five days:** train-the-trainer, Stage 0.5/0.65,
  CAO separation, eval design, blind review arms, qualification evidence, and
  practical assessment.

Credentials, if issued, bind a person to a named doctrine, policy, scenario,
rubric, date, and residual-gap record. Attendance alone is not competence.
